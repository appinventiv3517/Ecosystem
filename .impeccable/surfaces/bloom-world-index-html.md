# Bloom World homepage

Scope: the Bloom World homepage prototype (bloom-world/index.html). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: stakeholders reviewing the redesign; designed for home buyers/renters, residents, owners, brokers and families meeting Bloom.
Job: understand that Bloom is seven businesses running one community, then find a home, explore a community, reach a service, or enquire.
Proof: RFP figures, awards, story milestones, community and app facts in PRODUCT.md; stand-in imagery from the review prototype.
Constraints: prototype honesty (search/form not connected), EN/AR with RTL, Bloom wordmark and Bloom red for main actions, reduced motion.
Memorable moment: tapping a plot on the living masterplan draws Bloom's service lines to every business it works with, while the day clock runs the plan from 07:00 to night.
Unresolved: final photography, live listings, real Arabic copy review.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is Bloom's own masterplan, drawn live: an illustrated site plan where every Bloom business is a plot you can tap, and the plan runs through a day. It refuses the category page: a hero render, a search bar over it, and a row of community cards.

OWN-WORLD: Planning-drawing material at page scale. A saturated desert-sand site ground, zoning fields that own whole sections (residential ochre, education blue, hospitality coral, retail rose, healthcare mint, facilities slate, landscape green, lagoon turquoise), white roads with dashed centre lines, plot outlines, north arrows, scale bars, legends and a drawing title block. Bricolage Grotesque for display, Readex Pro for text and Arabic. Bloom red for every main action. Controls look like plan legend chips and stamped approvals.

STORY: A visitor sees one community working (homes, school, hotel, shops, care, facilities, parks), taps any plot and sees how it connects to the others, believes Bloom builds whole lives rather than units, and acts: searches homes in the plan legend, opens a community on the coast map, picks their app, or enquires.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The full-bleed animated masterplan fills the screen with cars, boats and people moving. Top left, a title block holds the wordmark lockup, the H1 "Welcome to Bloom World" and one line. Bottom left, the legend panel is the working home search (Buy/Rent, community, type, Search, in Bloom red). Bottom right, the day clock scrubber (07:00 to 24/7). Plots carry legend labels; the hint says "Tap a plot".
