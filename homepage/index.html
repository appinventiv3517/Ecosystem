Our Story

From one community to Bloom World.

2008

  1. 2008

    Bloom Gardens begins

    The first phase of Bloom Gardens launches: villas and townhouses inspired by the architecture of the Mediterranean.

  2. 2011

    Brighton College Al Ain

    Bloom Education launches Brighton College Al Ain, the second sister school of Brighton College UK in the UAE.

  3. 2012

    First homes handed over

    Phase One of Bloom Gardens is handed over to its owners.

  4. 2015

    Soho Square on Saadiyat

    Soho Square launches on Saadiyat Island: 304 homes, from studios to three-bedroom apartments, green houses and a sky villa.

  5. 2017

    Bloom Gardens grows

    Phase Three of Bloom Gardens is completed and handed over: 147 villas and townhouses.

  6. 2018

    Schools open in Dubai

    Brighton College Dubai opens its doors, and Bloom Education opens Dwight School’s first campus in the Middle East.

  7. 2022

    Bloom Living launches

    Cordoba, the first phase of Bloom Living in Zayed City, launches in March and sells out within four hours.

  8. 2024

    Three more phases

    Seville, Olvera and Almeria, the fifth to seventh phases of Bloom Living, launch with lakeside villas and townhouses.

  9. 2026

    Bloom Arjaan by Rotana opens

    The 217-key residential-style hotel opens on Saadiyat Island, adding to Bloom’s hospitality portfolio.
