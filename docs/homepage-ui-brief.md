# Bloom Holding — Homepage UI/UX Design & Interactive Experience

Act as a world-class Senior Product Designer, Creative Director, UX Architect and Frontend Engineer.

We have completed the Product Discovery phase for the Bloom Holding website redesign. I now want to move into the UI design and frontend implementation phase.

IMPORTANT: Start a NEW design conversation, but continue working within the existing project codebase. Read the finalized discovery file attached to this conversation before writing any code.

Reference file: `Bloom Homepage Product Discovery.html`

This is the approved discovery document after multiple feedback and design iterations. Treat it as the primary source of truth. Do not repeat the discovery phase or change its conclusions.

Our immediate scope is strictly the Bloom Holding homepage. Do not build the entire website or individual inner pages.

## 1. Project Understanding

Bloom Holding is a diversified master developer with seven business verticals:

1. Real Estate
2. Education
3. Hospitality
4. Retail
5. Healthcare
6. Facilities Management
7. Landscaping

The brand proposition is:

**Curated Community Living**

The homepage should become the main gateway to the Bloom ecosystem, connecting the master brand, communities, properties and customer services.

It must cater to four audience groups:

* Buyers and Renters
* Existing Residents and Customers
* Investors and Business Partners
* Corporate Visitors and Stakeholders

The four primary homepage destinations are:

* Find a Home
* Explore Communities
* Customer Services
* About Bloom

## 2. Discovery Findings — Must Be Reflected in the Design

The finalized discovery identified six important pain points.

High Priority

1. Unclear Brand Positioning
2. Limited Visibility of User Journeys
3. Fragmented Property Discovery

Medium Priority

4. Disconnected Ecosystem Storytelling
5. Limited Community Discovery
6. Customer Service Discoverability

The five agreed UX opportunities are:

1. Stronger Brand Introduction
2. Intent-Led Homepage Navigation
3. Prominent Property & Community Discovery
4. Connected Ecosystem Storytelling
5. Improved Customer Access & Engagement

The new homepage must address these problems through its structure, content hierarchy, interactions and visual experience.

Do not simply make the existing website look more attractive. Rethink the experience around user intent.

## 3. Visual Design Direction — Very Important

I want the website to feel premium, expressive, playful and immersive.

The client specifically expects a highly interactive website with rich animations and engaging motion throughout the experience.

Think of a premium real estate brand combined with the creativity of an award-winning digital agency.

### Brand identity

Strictly follow the branding used in the attached finalized discovery deck.

Use:

* The exact Bloom Holding logo/wordmark embedded in the reference HTML.
* The same deep brick-red brand accent used in the deck: `#962B23`.
* Warm cream background: `#F8F6EA`.
* Deep charcoal/black: `#0B0B0B`.
* Supporting warm beige, soft ivory and subtle neutral tones.

The cream background should be the dominant canvas throughout the experience.

The red should be used intentionally for CTAs, highlights, interactive elements and typography accents.

Do not introduce an unrelated color palette. Do not recreate, distort, recolor or replace the Bloom logo.

### Typography

Use the typography direction from the approved discovery deck:

* DM Sans for navigation, body text, labels and UI.
* Playfair Display for selected large editorial headlines and expressive brand statements.

Use sophisticated typography, generous spacing and strong visual hierarchy.

### Overall feeling

* Premium, not corporate and boring.
* Playful, not childish.
* Interactive, not visually overwhelming.
* Editorial, not template-driven.
* Motion-rich, not chaotic.
* Modern, elegant and memorable.

Avoid conventional real estate website layouts with repetitive rectangular cards and standard blue corporate buttons.

## 4. Homepage Structure & Design Requirements

Build a complete, vertically scrollable homepage.

Use this proposed experience architecture as the starting point.

### SECTION 01 — Navigation & Header

Create a premium, elegant navigation experience.

Include:

* Bloom logo on the left.
* Find a Home.
* Explore Communities.
* Customer Services.
* About Bloom.
* Language selector: EN / AR.
* A prominent enquiry or contact action.

Interactions:

* Sticky header with smooth transformation on scrolling.
* Beautiful dropdown or mega-menu animations.
* Animated navigation indicators.
* Smooth hover transitions.
* Subtle logo interaction.
* Elegant mobile navigation with full-screen animated menu.
* Active section indicators where appropriate.

The header should feel light, spacious and premium.

### SECTION 02 — Immersive Hero Experience

This is the most important section.

Create a memorable hero experience that immediately communicates Bloom's proposition.

Main headline:

**Curated Community Living**

Supporting copy:

"Discover thoughtfully curated places, experiences and communities that bring everyday living together."

Primary CTAs:

* Find a Home
* Explore Communities

Visual direction:

* Large cinematic residential community imagery.
* Architectural photography, landscape elements and premium residential visuals.
* Full-width visual treatment.
* Layered typography.
* Elegant overlays and depth.
* Strong composition with sufficient readability.

Animation ideas:

* Hero image cinematic zoom-in.
* Text reveal using masking and staggered animation.
* Animated line drawing or organic graphic elements.
* Subtle parallax effects.
* Floating architectural or landscape elements.
* Smooth CTA entrance animations.
* Animated scroll indicator.
* Background image transitions where appropriate.

The hero should immediately communicate that Bloom is about more than individual properties. It is about the complete living experience.

Make the hero visually exciting without compromising the brand identity.

### SECTION 03 — Find a Home

Create a prominent and interactive property discovery experience.

This section must address the discovery finding around fragmented sales and leasing journeys.

Headline:

**Find a place to call home.**

Include a premium search component with:

* Buy / Rent toggle.
* Location or community selector.
* Property type selector.
* Bedroom selection.
* Search CTA.

Use animated transitions when switching between Buy and Rent.

Design a beautiful search experience with:

* Animated tab indicator.
* Expanding input interactions.
* Custom dropdowns.
* Elegant focus states.
* Interactive selection feedback.
* Smooth hover effects.

Make the search module stand out using a contrasting cream/white surface.

Do not invent real property inventory or live availability. Use existing approved assets and clearly identifiable demo content if necessary.

* Horizontal drag/swipe.

* Image zoom on hover.

Use real Bloom community assets available in the project or the supplied discovery HTML. Do not invent project names.

### SECTION 06 — Life at Bloom / Community Living

## 5. Animation & Interaction Requirements

This is a major creative requirement.

I want animations throughout the website, not just basic fade-in effects.

Use different animation techniques in different sections.

### Animation library

First inspect the existing project dependencies.

Prefer Framer Motion or GSAP if compatible with the current setup. Use CSS animations and Intersection Observer where they offer a simpler, more performant approach.

Do not unnecessarily change the existing project architecture.

### Required motion experiences

Page loading

* Smooth initial reveal.
* Logo or brand-led intro.
* Staggered navigation entrance.
* Hero content reveal.

Navigation

* Animated dropdowns.
* Magnetic or responsive CTA hover effects.
* Underline and active-state transitions.
* Smooth mobile menu transitions.

Hero

* Cinematic image zoom.
* Masked text reveals.
* Staggered headline animations.
* Parallax.
* Floating visual elements.

Scrolling

* Scroll-triggered section entrances.
* Image reveals and masks.
* Parallax backgrounds.
* Staggered content animation.
* Animated progress indicators.
* Smooth section transitions.

Cards and images

* Image scale and crop transitions.
* Subtle 3D tilt where appropriate.
* Interactive hover depth.
* Animated borders and overlays.

Property search

* Animated Buy/Rent switch.
* Smooth dropdown transitions.
* Input focus interactions.
* Selection feedback.

Community carousel

* Drag interaction.
* Smooth inertia.
* Animated active slide.
* Image depth and movement.

Business ecosystem

* Animated connections.
* Interactive highlighting.
* Content transitions.
* Hover and click feedback.

Statistics

* Count-up animation.
* Number reveal.
* Subtle scroll-based motion.

Footer

* Link hover transitions.
* Animated arrows.
* Smooth entrance.

Use varied motion styles to make the experience feel thoughtfully art-directed.

Avoid repeating the same fade-up animation for every single element.

### Important motion principles

* Animations should feel smooth and intentional.
* Avoid excessive bouncing and gimmicky movements.
* Use performant transform and opacity animations.
* Keep text readable during motion.
* Do not make interaction depend exclusively on hover.
* Support mobile touch interactions.
* Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
* Ensure the website remains usable without animations.
