An initial review of how the existing homepage communicates Bloom’s brand, services and user journeys. Sources: Website RFP pp. 2–4 and a preliminary audit of the live homepage (bloomholding.com, captured 12 Nov 2026, desktop 1440px). Screenshots are genuine captures. The hero background video did not load in the capture environment, so the first view shows the wordmark on black; re-capture in a normal browser before client use. Audit questions per dimension: 1 First Impression & Brand Proposition: Does the homepage immediately communicate who Bloom Holding is? Is its master developer positioning clear? Is Curated Community Living meaningfully explained? 2 Hero Section & Primary CTA: Does the hero serve different audience needs? Are key user actions immediately visible? Does the primary CTA reflect the breadth of Bloom’s offering? 3 Navigation & Information Hierarchy: Can users easily identify where to go? Are important journeys easily discoverable? Is navigation based on user intent or business structure? 4 Property & Community Discovery: How easily can users find a home? Are Buy and Rent pathways visible? Can users explore communities without unnecessary navigation? 5 Business Ecosystem: Are the verticals clearly represented? Is the relationship between businesses and community living explained? 6 Customer Services: Can existing customers and residents easily identify their service entry points?
