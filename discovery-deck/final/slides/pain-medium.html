The second half of “Where does the current experience fall short?”: three pain points we rate as preliminary medium priority. 4 Disconnected Ecosystem Storytelling: Bloom’s business verticals are not sufficiently connected through one consistent expression of Curated Community Living. User impact: visitors may not understand how Bloom’s businesses collectively contribute to its communities. (RFP p.2 A) 5 Limited Community Discovery: community storytelling and active property opportunities are not sufficiently connected. User impact: users may struggle to move naturally from exploring a community to finding relevant properties. (RFP p.3 C) 6 Customer Service Discoverability: the homepage needs stronger pathways for existing residents and customers to access relevant services. User impact: important post-purchase and resident service journeys may be less accessible. (RFP pp. 2, 6) The preliminary live-site audit is consistent with all six. User impacts are hypotheses, not validated user feedback. High and Medium are a preliminary view for discussion, to be confirmed with stakeholders, research and analytics.
