RFP pp. 5–6 · Areas to explore, not design solutions

The discovery findings point towards five key areas of homepage experience improvement. Sources: Website RFP pp. 5–6. The five opportunity areas map to the RFP ambition: differentiate the master brand through evidence; restructure around user intent; make the homepage the gateway to the Bloom ecosystem; unify property discovery; improve customer access to services. They are areas to explore, not UI solutions. In full: 1 Stronger Brand Introduction: establish Bloom’s identity, scale and unique proposition immediately. 2 Intent-Led Homepage Navigation: make important destinations accessible according to visitor needs. 3 Prominent Property & Community Discovery: create clearer entry points to finding homes and exploring communities. 4 Connected Ecosystem Storytelling: demonstrate how Bloom’s business verticals collectively support its community living proposition. 5 Improved Customer Access & Engagement: make customer services, enquiries and important next steps easier to identify.
