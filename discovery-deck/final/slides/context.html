Project context in full: Bloom Holding is a diversified master developer with businesses across Real Estate, Education, Hospitality, Retail, Healthcare, Facilities Management and Landscaping (the seven arches). The current website needs to evolve into a connected digital ecosystem that communicates Bloom’s proposition while serving the needs of its different audiences. Discovery objective: evaluate the current homepage experience and identify opportunities to (1) communicate Bloom’s brand proposition more clearly, (2) improve discoverability of key user journeys, (3) connect Bloom’s business ecosystem, (4) make property and community discovery more accessible, (5) improve access to relevant customer services. Scope: homepage discovery and UX assessment only. Source: Website RFP pp. 1, 4–6.
