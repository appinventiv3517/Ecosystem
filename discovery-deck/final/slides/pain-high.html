Where does the current experience fall short? The initial discovery highlights gaps in brand communication, navigation, discoverability and audience relevance. These three are our preliminary high priorities. 1 Unclear Brand Positioning: the homepage does not immediately communicate Bloom’s complete identity, scale and differentiated value proposition. User impact: first-time visitors may not fully understand what Bloom offers beyond its property developments. (RFP p.2 A) 2 Limited Visibility of User Journeys: the experience does not give sufficient prominence to the key actions visitors want to accomplish. User impact: buyers, renters and residents may need to explore navigation before reaching their intended destination. (RFP p.2 B) 3 Fragmented Property Discovery: sales and leasing journeys are not sufficiently unified into an intuitive discovery experience. User impact: finding the right property can involve unnecessary navigation and disconnected journeys. (RFP p.3 B/C) User impacts are hypotheses, not validated user feedback. Priorities are a preliminary view for discussion.
