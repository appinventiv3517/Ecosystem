Preliminary observations from the live homepage, desktop only · RFP pp. 2–4

Key observation: the current experience is primarily organization-led, while Bloom’s ambition is to make it more user-led. Observations in full (preliminary, desktop only, from the live homepage captured 12 Nov 2026): 1 The first view shows the Bloom wordmark only. “Curated Community Living” appears on scroll as a heading without explanation; the master developer statement sits further down. 2 One primary action, Search Properties, with Make Enquiry in the header. No visible entry point for residents, investors or corporate visitors. 3 Who We Are, What We Do, Properties, Media Centre, Careers. Labels follow the company structure. 4 Search filters by property, type and bedrooms, with no Buy or Rent choice. Sales and Leasing are separate menu items; 13 developments are listed by name. 5 Five businesses appear as numbered tiles (Properties, Education, Hospitality, Landscape, Facilities Management). Retail and Healthcare are not shown, and the link to community living is not explained. 6 No resident or customer entry point in the header; the Bloom apps sit under “E-Services” in the footer. Mobile, Arabic and analytics review are still to come.
