RFP pp. 2, 5–6 · Audience needs are working assumptions to validate in research

One homepage, multiple user intentions. The homepage needs to accommodate different audiences without requiring them to understand Bloom’s organizational structure. Key insight: different audiences arrive with different expectations. The homepage should provide clear entry points based on what visitors want to accomplish. The four pills are the four primary homepage destinations named in the RFP (p. 5): Find a Home, Explore Communities, Customer Services, About Bloom. The red italic line in each arch is that audience’s primary intention. Sources: Website RFP pp. 2, 5–6. The RFP names the audiences (buyers, investors, brokers, residents, partners, corporate stakeholders, media, employees). The four groupings and the needs listed are our working assumptions for discovery; they have not yet been validated with users. Personas are an RFP discovery deliverable (p. 6). Needs in full. Buyers & Renters: find available properties, explore residential communities, understand property details, submit an enquiry. Existing Residents & Customers: access customer services, find relevant support, access community-related services. Investors & Business Partners: understand Bloom’s business portfolio, explore its scale and capabilities, evaluate its credibility and track record. Corporate Visitors & Stakeholders: understand Bloom’s identity, explore its business verticals, discover leadership, news and company information.
