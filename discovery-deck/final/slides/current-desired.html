The homepage should not simply introduce Bloom Holding. It should help every visitor understand Bloom and identify their next meaningful action.

The opportunity is to transform the homepage from a corporate introduction into a connected gateway to Bloom’s ecosystem. Current experience in full. Brand communication: broad corporate messaging; limited immediate differentiation. Navigation: primarily organization-led; users need to understand business categories. User journeys: important actions are not sufficiently prominent; different journeys require navigation exploration. Property discovery: sales and leasing journeys lack a unified experience. Business ecosystem: business verticals are presented as separate organizational categories. Customer services: customer-facing pathways need greater homepage prominence. Desired experience in full. Brand communication: clear positioning; evidence-led Curated Community Living proposition. Navigation: user-intent-led structure; clear and accessible navigation. User journeys: prominent entry points for priority audiences. Property discovery: unified Find a Home experience with clear Buy and Rent pathways. Business ecosystem: connected expression of Bloom’s businesses and capabilities. Customer services: easy access to relevant customer and resident services. Key takeaway: the homepage should not simply introduce Bloom Holding. It should help every visitor understand Bloom and identify their next meaningful action. Sources: “Current” summarises RFP-stated challenges (pp. 2–4) and the preliminary live-site audit. “Desired” reflects the RFP ambition (pp. 4–6). This is a direction of travel, not a layout proposal.
