## Product Purpose

Bloom Holding is a diversified master developer in Abu Dhabi and Dubai with seven businesses: Real Estate (Bloom Properties), Education (Bloom Education), Hospitality (Bloom Hospitality), Retail, Healthcare, Facilities Management (Bloom Facilities Management) and Landscaping (Bloom Landscape). The work in this repo makes the case that these businesses should read as one connected "Bloom World" with a shared design language, while each product keeps its own job.

The homepage's job: introduce Bloom World, let people find a home (buy or rent), explore the five communities, reach customer services and the three apps, and make an enquiry.

## Positioning

Bloom is not one developer selling units. It is seven businesses that make one community work together: the home, the school run, the facilities team, the shops, care, the landscape, and the hotel for visiting family. The tagline in use is "Curated Community Living".

## Product Principles

1. One Bloom, many businesses: every surface should show how the seven connect, not list them.
2. Get people to their task: finding a home, reaching a service and enquiring are never more than one move away.
3. Prove with Bloom's own facts and places, never generic claims.
4. Honest prototype: anything not live is labelled.
